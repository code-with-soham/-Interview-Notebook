## 18.1 Core HR Questions (The Classics)

### 📋 Interview Questions
1. Tell me about yourself. *(Elevator Pitch: Who you are, what you know, what you've built, what you're looking for).*
2. Why do you want to work for this company specifically?
3. What do you know about our company and our products?
4. Why should we hire you over other candidates with similar backgrounds?
5. What are your greatest professional strengths?
6. What is your greatest weakness? *(Provide a real weakness and actionable steps you are taking to improve it).*
7. Where do you see yourself in 3 years? In 5 years?
8. What is your expected salary?
9. Are you willing to relocate?
10. Are you open to working night shifts or weekends if required?
11. What are your hobbies outside of programming?
12. Describe your ideal work environment. Do you prefer remote, hybrid, or in-office?

## 18.2 The STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

### ✅ Important Topics
- [ ] Structuring answers using STAR
- [ ] Quantifying results (metrics, percentages, time saved)
- [ ] Focusing on "I" instead of "We"
- [ ] Keeping answers concise (2-3 minutes max)

### 📋 Interview Questions (Answer these using STAR)
1. Tell me about a time you faced a significant technical challenge. How did you overcome it?
2. Tell me about a time you failed or made a mistake. What did you learn?
3. Describe a situation where you had a conflict with a team member or peer. How was it resolved?
4. Tell me about a time you had to work under a very tight deadline.
5. Give an example of a goal you reached and tell me how you achieved it.
6. Tell me about a time you went above and beyond expectations for a project.
7. Describe a time when you had to learn a completely new technology very quickly to complete a task.
8. Tell me about a time you received constructive criticism. How did you react?
9. Have you ever strongly disagreed with a technical decision made by your team/professor? How did you handle it?
10. Tell me about a time you took leadership on a project, even if you weren't the designated leader.

## 18.3 Technical & Work-Style Behavioral Questions

### 📋 Interview Questions
1. How do you keep your technical skills sharp? How do you stay updated with industry trends?
2. What is your process for debugging a complex issue?
3. How do you review someone else's code? What specific things do you look for?
4. Do you prefer working independently or in a team? Why?
5. How do you handle context switching when assigned multiple tasks at once?
6. Describe a situation where you had to explain a complex technical concept to a non-technical person.
7. Tell me about a time your code broke in production or during a demo. What was your immediate reaction?
8. What does "clean code" mean to you?
9. How do you decide when a piece of code is "good enough" vs when it needs to be refactored?
10. Tell me about a time you realized you were not going to meet a deadline. How did you communicate this?

## 18.4 Motivation & Culture Fit

### 📋 Interview Questions
1. What are you most passionate about in software engineering?
2. What is your favorite programming language and why? What do you dislike about it?
3. Tell me about a side project you are working on just for fun.
4. Who are your role models in the tech industry?
5. If you could change one thing about your college experience, what would it be?
6. What kind of management style do you thrive under?
7. How do you handle working with a difficult or uncooperative team member?
8. What motivates you to do your best work?
9. Tell me about a time you felt overwhelmed by work/studies. How did you handle the stress?
10. If you were given a task but the requirements were extremely vague, what would you do?

## 18.5 Reverse Interviewing (Questions for the Interviewer)

### ✅ Important Topics
- [ ] Never say "I have no questions."
- [ ] Ask questions that show you are evaluating them as much as they are evaluating you.

### 📋 Suggested Questions
1. What does a typical day look like for a Junior Developer on this team?
2. What is the current tech stack, and are there plans to adopt any new technologies in the near future?
3. How is mentorship and onboarding handled for freshers joining the team?
4. What is the most exciting engineering challenge your team is currently trying to solve?
5. How do you measure success for this role in the first 3 to 6 months?
6. Can you walk me through the code review and deployment process here?
7. What are the opportunities for professional growth and learning within the company?
8. How does the engineering team balance building new features versus paying off technical debt?
9. What do you personally enjoy most about working at this company?
10. What are the next steps in the interview process?

---

### 🎯 What Interviewer Expects (Behavioral)
- [ ] **Communication Skills:** Can you articulate your thoughts clearly without rambling?
- [ ] **Self-Awareness:** Can you admit mistakes and show a growth mindset?
- [ ] **Passion for Tech:** Do you code outside of required coursework? Do you build things?
- [ ] **Culture Fit:** Will you be easy to work with? Are you coachable?
- [ ] **Honesty:** Saying "I don't know, but here is how I would find out" instead of making up an answer.

### ❌ Common Mistakes (Behavioral)
- [ ] Lying or exaggerating your role on a team project.
- [ ] Speaking negatively about past professors, peers, or employers.
- [ ] Giving generic, fake answers to "What is your weakness?" (e.g., "I work too hard" or "I care too much").
- [ ] Not preparing questions to ask at the end of the interview.
- [ ] Failing to use the STAR method, resulting in unstructured, hard-to-follow stories.
