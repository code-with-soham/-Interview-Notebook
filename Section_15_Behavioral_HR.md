## 15.1 The STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

### ✅ Important Topics
- [ ] Structuring answers using STAR
- [ ] Quantifying results (metrics, percentages, time saved)
- [ ] Focusing on "I" instead of "We"
- [ ] Keeping answers concise (2-3 minutes max)
- [ ] Preparing 3-4 versatile core stories

### 📋 Interview Preparation Checklist
1. Identify a story where you solved a difficult technical problem.
2. Identify a story where you had a conflict with a team member or peer and resolved it.
3. Identify a story where you failed or made a mistake, and what you learned.
4. Identify a story where you went above and beyond expectations.
5. Identify a story where you had to learn a new technology very quickly.

## 15.2 Common HR Questions (Fresher Focus)

### 📋 Interview Questions
1. Tell me about yourself. *(Elevator Pitch: Who you are, what you know, what you've built, what you're looking for).*
2. Why do you want to work for this company?
3. What are your greatest strengths?
4. What is your greatest weakness? *(Provide a real weakness and the steps you are taking to improve it).*
5. Where do you see yourself in 3 to 5 years?
6. Why should we hire you over other candidates?
7. What are your salary expectations?
8. Are you willing to relocate?
9. How do you handle working under pressure or strict deadlines?

## 15.3 Technical Behavioral Questions

### 📋 Interview Questions
1. Tell me about the most challenging project you have worked on. What was your role?
2. Describe a time when you were stuck on a bug for hours or days. How did you eventually solve it?
3. How do you keep your technical skills sharp? How do you learn about new technologies?
4. Tell me about a time you had to learn a completely new framework or language in a short amount of time to complete a project.
5. Have you ever strongly disagreed with a technical decision made by your team/professor/peer? How did you handle it?
6. Describe a situation where you had to explain a complex technical concept to a non-technical person.
7. Tell me about a time your code broke in production or during a demo. What did you do?
8. How do you review someone else's code? What do you look for?
9. Do you prefer working independently or in a team? Why?
10. Tell me about a time you realized you were not going to meet a deadline. How did you communicate this?

## 15.4 Project-Specific Questions

### 📋 Interview Questions (Be prepared to answer these for EVERY project on your resume)
1. What was the core problem this project was trying to solve?
2. Why did you choose the MERN stack (or specific DB/framework) for this project instead of alternatives?
3. Walk me through the architecture of this application.
4. What was the hardest technical challenge you faced while building this, and how did you overcome it?
5. If you had 2 more months to work on this project, what features would you add or improve?
6. How did you handle state management in the frontend?
7. How did you design the database schema? Why did you structure it that way?
8. How did you secure the application? (Authentication, Authorization, Data Validation).
9. Is this project deployed? How is it hosted? What challenges did you face during deployment?
10. How does the AI/LLM integration work in this project? What specific prompts or APIs did you use?

## 15.5 Questions to Ask the Interviewer (Reverse Interviewing)

### ✅ Important Topics
- [ ] Never say "I have no questions."
- [ ] Ask about the tech stack, team culture, and expectations.

### 📋 Suggested Questions
1. What does a typical day look like for a Junior Developer on this team?
2. What is the current tech stack, and are there plans to adopt any new technologies in the near future?
3. How is mentorship and onboarding handled for new freshers joining the team?
4. What is the most exciting engineering challenge your team is currently trying to solve?
5. How do you measure success for this role in the first 3 to 6 months?
6. What is the code review and deployment process like here?

---

### 🎯 What Interviewer Expects (Behavioral)
- [ ] **Communication Skills:** Can you articulate your thoughts clearly without rambling?
- [ ] **Self-Awareness:** Can you admit mistakes and show a growth mindset?
- [ ] **Passion for Tech:** Do you code outside of required coursework? Do you build things?
- [ ] **Culture Fit:** Will you be easy to work with? Are you coachable?
- [ ] **Ownership:** Can you talk in-depth about the projects on your resume, proving you actually wrote the code?

### ❌ Common Mistakes (Behavioral)
- [ ] Lying or exaggerating your role on a team project.
- [ ] Speaking negatively about past professors, peers, or employers.
- [ ] Giving generic answers to "What is your weakness?" (e.g., "I work too hard" or "I'm a perfectionist").
- [ ] Not preparing questions to ask at the end of the interview.
- [ ] Failing to use the STAR method, resulting in unstructured, hard-to-follow stories.
